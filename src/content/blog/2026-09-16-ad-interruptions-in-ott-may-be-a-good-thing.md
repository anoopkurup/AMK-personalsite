---
title: Ad interruptions in OTT may be a good thing
date: 2026-09-16T20:41:00.000+05:30
---
I was watching a series on Amazon Prime when an ad interrupted me.

Gave me a much-needed break. Got off the chair. Drank some water. Decided the series wasn't interesting enough to binge-watch.

The ads are giving our hijacked attention a much-needed break. And also help us do something useful instead of binge-watching another badly written show.

They should charge more for accounts with ad breaks.  :)
